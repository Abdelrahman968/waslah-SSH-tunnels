Terms of Service

            

              Last updated: 
            

            

              

                1. What Waslah is. Waslah is a desktop client
                for connecting through SSH tunnels you already have credentials
                for, with optional SNI-based TLS fronting. It is provided as-is,
                without warranty of any kind.
              

              

                2. Your responsibility for network compliance.
                Using SSH tunneling or SNI fronting to route around a specific
                network's traffic filtering may be against that network's or
                your ISP's terms of service, depending on your country and
                provider. Waslah does not perform any unauthorized access to
                systems — it connects to servers you already have credentials
                for — but compliance with your local ISP's terms is your
                responsibility, not ours.
              

              

                3. No guarantee of service. Network tools,
                hotspot sharing, and every other feature are provided on a
                best-effort basis and can change at any time.
              

              

                4. Changes. These terms can be updated;
                continued use after an update means you accept the revised
                terms.
              

            

          


          

            
Privacy Policy

            

              Last updated: 
            

            

              

                1. Everything is stored locally, on your device.
                Your SSH profiles, SNI list, connection logs, and settings are
                stored locally in an encrypted local file (tied to your Windows
                account via the OS's credential storage). This data never leaves
                your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.
              

              

                2. No cloud, no accounts. Waslah doesn't create
                any account for you, doesn't send your data to any server, and
                has no backend of its own. The only network calls it makes are
                the SSH/SNI connections you configure, and the diagnostic tools
                you run yourself (DNS lookup, ping, speed test, etc.), plus
                checking the Windows internet-sharing settings when you use that
                feature.
              

              

                3. What we don't do. We don't sell your data,
                run ads, or share anything with third parties — there's nothing
                of yours to share.
              

              

                4. Your choices. You can delete all local data
                at any time (Settings > Reset), or export/delete individual
                profiles whenever you want.
              

              

                5. Changes. This policy can be updated;
                material changes will be reflected here with a new "last
                updated" date.
